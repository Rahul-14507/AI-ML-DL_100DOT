```
## Week N

Days completed this week (out of 7):
Quiz score:

Three things I can now do that I could not do last Sunday:
1.
2.
3.

One topic I still do not understand:

One thing that got in the way this week:

What I will change next week:
```

If the "three things" are hard to write, the week was probably spent watching rather than doing. Use the buffer day to build something small that proves one of them.
